# Legal & IP Statement

This document details the Intellectual Property (IP) ownership, license alignment, and compliance policies for KQLBridge.

## 1. Proprietary Independence & Clean-Room Architecture
All compiler components inside KQLBridge—including but not limited to the Lark-based frontend parser, target dialect emitters (Spark SQL, T-SQL, PySpark, DuckDB), the middle-end semantic validator, and scope management frameworks—have been developed completely independently from clean-room specifications. No proprietary code, internal enterprise codebases, or copyrighted non-public algorithms have been used in the creation of KQLBridge.

## 2. Open-Source Contributions
Contributors agree that all submissions made to this repository are licensed under the terms of the project's standard `LICENSE` (Apache 2.0 or MIT, as per the root LICENSE file) and that they waive any conflicting proprietary claims to compiled translation mappings, test convergence suites, and diagnostic artifacts.
